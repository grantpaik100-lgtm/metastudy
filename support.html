Connection troubleshooting

  1. Confirm that StudyMeta is enabled in the AI client's plugin or tools menu.
  2. Reconnect StudyMeta and complete OAuth authorization with the intended email account.
  3. Start a new chat and try 내 학습 상태를 보여줘.
  4. If the problem continues, include the approximate time, client name, and visible error message in your support email. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

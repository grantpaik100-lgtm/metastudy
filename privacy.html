Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

1. About StudyMeta

StudyMeta is a learner-context service operated by the StudyMeta Team. It helps an AI learning assistant retrieve an authenticated learner's profile, current learning state, and recent learning evidence, and record new learning events.

2. Information we process

  • Account information such as email address, authentication user ID, and authorization status.
  • Learner profile preferences, including preferred language, pace, explanation depth, and interaction style.
  • Domain and skill state, including mastery, retrieval, transfer, help-need, confidence, and misconception fields.
  • Learning events and evidence intentionally sent to StudyMeta, including task descriptions, outcomes, hint usage, and timestamps.
  • Limited technical information needed for authentication, security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

When StudyMeta is used from an AI client, the client may send relevant content from the current request to a StudyMeta tool. StudyMeta does not request unrelated chat history.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, link them to the correct learner record, provide personalized learning context, record requested learning events, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Storage and service providers

StudyMeta currently uses Supabase for authentication and database services and Vercel for application hosting. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services. We do not sell personal information or use learner data for advertising.

5. Access and sharing

Authenticated access is restricted to the learner record linked to the signed-in account. We may share information with infrastructure providers, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service. We do not share learner data with unrelated third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain learner information while the account or service relationship is active and as needed for security and legal obligations. You may request access, correction, account unlinking, or deletion by contacting grantpaik100@gmail.com. We will verify the requester before acting on the request.

7. Security

We use OAuth authorization, row-level database access controls, encrypted network connections, and restricted server credentials. No system is perfectly secure, so users should not submit secrets or unnecessary sensitive information as learning evidence.

8. Children and schools

StudyMeta is not intended for independent use by children under 13. Where a learner is a minor, a parent, guardian, school, or other authorized adult must provide any consent required by applicable law.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as StudyMeta changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published. Questions can be sent to grantpaik100@gmail.com.
